Frozen plans and complete native outputs are retained as local generated artifacts under `out/optimization-20261005/`; exact raw hashes and per-case judgments are in [manual.json](manual.json). All diagnostic arms are complete. Subsequent real-QA validation is recorded separately for [F original](../bugfix-20261005-f-known-original/REVIEW.md), [F authority](../bugfix-20261005-f-known-authority/REVIEW.md), and [G precision follow-up](../bugfix-20261005-g-known-original/REVIEW.md). These diagnostic findings alone are not a production recommendation.

The planned-inspection value cites its actual table; the conflicting deadline answer safely gives both unapproved alternatives. The current-fee answer chooses the correct rule but incompletely attaches its governing continuation clause. Equivalent units, supplied code and same-cohort totals still produce incorrect or internally contradictory conclusions.

Candidate B generates evidence, a comparison, status and final claims in one structured call. **Five outputs fail its original structural parser. One is structurally accepted but falsely says the supplied duration is absent.** All six choose an empty evidence array, which the original grammar permits. Empty evidence alone does not invalidate the original `missing` status when every claim also has no sources. The stricter C parser rejects all six B outputs under its new contract; that must not be represented as B's original acceptance rule. B safely handles the same-cohort uncertainty, but falsely refuses an approved current fee, omits the requested code results and provides no reliable overall replacement.

Grammar acceptance and quotation presence are mechanical properties. They do not establish claim entailment, correct authority, arithmetic, completeness or absence of contradictory conclusions. Every native failure and unhelpful result remains in the record.

Candidate C enforces nonempty evidence and bounded strings in its grammar, and appends deterministic unit/code arithmetic annotations without changing source text. It produces **2 strict passes of 6 requested cases**, plus one supported answer rejected for a quotation line break, one incorrect citation with incomplete requested detail, one timeout and one unobserved case. The original deadline and table answers pass. The units answer correctly concludes that 90 seconds equals 1.5 minutes, yet attaches unrelated passage 4:4 rather than its selected 9:9/10:10 evidence, and omits the original 1.5 minutes from its visible final claim. The code request exceeds 180 seconds without a raw answer; the runner terminates its owned worker, leaving the final same-cohort case unmeasured. It is not counted as a semantic failure or success, and there is no retry.

C's completed answers take 113–158 seconds; its code timeout is an additional operational failure. Its original parser result is retained separately as `mechanical-frozen-parser.json`. Any later whitespace-only anchoring revision must be reported as a new parser interpretation of the same raw response; it cannot erase the frozen rejection or fix the unrelated units citation. These results do not justify enabling C as a reliable general answer path.
